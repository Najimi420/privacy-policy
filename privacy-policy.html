Privacy Policy for Salah Pro

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Salah Pro helps users access Islamic features, including prayer times. We respect user privacy and aim to handle information transparently.

2. Location Information

Salah Pro uses device location, with the user's permission, to provide location-based prayer times. Location access can be managed through the device's settings.

Location is used to provide prayer-time functionality and is not used by the developer for advertising or analytics. See the final published policy for details of whether location is processed locally or transmitted to a third-party service.

3. Personal Information

Salah Pro does not require users to create an account or provide their name, phone number, or email address to use its basic features.

4. Analytics and Advertising

The developer does not intentionally use analytics or advertising services in Salah Pro. Any third-party SDKs included in the app must be reviewed to confirm their actual data practices.

5. Third-Party Services and Diagnostics

Third-party services used by the app may process technical information as necessary to provide their functionality. The developer must disclose any crash-reporting or diagnostic services included in the published app.

6. Data Security and Retention

Information handling depends on how each feature and third-party service operates. We aim to limit data handling to what is necessary to provide the app's features.

7. Children's Privacy

Salah Pro does not intentionally request personal information from users through its basic features.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at this URL.
